Questions

Good to know

Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. Pay $10 once and Cyanote is yours forever, including all future updates. No subscription, no upsells.

How do I get it after paying?

Right after checkout you'll get an instant download link (and it's emailed to you too). Download, drag Cyanote to your Applications folder, and you're set — no license key to enter.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your own Mac. Cyanote is local-first — there's no account and nothing is uploaded to a server. You can even password-lock individual notes.

Which Macs are supported?

Any Apple Silicon Mac — M1 or later — running macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer. Intel and Windows versions are on the roadmap.

What's your refund policy?

If Cyanote isn't for you, email within 14 days for a full refund — no hard feelings.
