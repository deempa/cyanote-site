Questions.

How do I get it after paying?

A download link right after checkout, and by email, together with your licence key. Drag the app to Applications, open it, and paste the key once when it asks. There is no account to create, and the key is not checked again after that.

Where is my data stored?

On your own computer, in a local database. No account, nothing uploaded. Any note can be password-locked.

Which computers is it for?

macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer. Universal binary — Apple Silicon and Intel — notarized by Apple. Windows is on the way; the same $10 covers it.
