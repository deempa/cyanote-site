Is it really a one-time payment?

Yes. $10 once, and every future version is included. There is no subscription and nothing else to buy.

How do I get it after paying?

You'll get a download link straight after checkout, and by email. Drag Cyanote to your Applications folder and open it — the app itself needs no licence key to run. Keep the key from your receipt: it's what lets the app fetch updates later.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your own Mac, in a local database. There's no account and nothing is uploaded. Individual notes can be password-locked.

Which Macs are supported?

Any Apple Silicon Mac — M1 or later — running macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer. Intel and Windows are on the roadmap.
