Refund Policy

Last updated: July 2026

14-day money-back guarantee. If Cyanote isn't right for you, email us within 14 days of your purchase and we'll refund you in full — no hard feelings and no need to explain.

How to request a refund

Email support@cyanote.app from the address you used to buy, or include your Lemon Squeezy order number. We aim to reply within a couple of business days.

How refunds are processed

Purchases are handled by our reseller, Lemon Squeezy (our Merchant of Record). Approved refunds are issued back to your original payment method through Lemon Squeezy, and can also be requested via the receipt they email you.

After 14 days

Because Cyanote is a one-time purchase with free lifetime updates, we generally don't offer refunds after the 14-day window — but if something's genuinely wrong (for example the app won't run on a supported Mac), reach out anyway and we'll make it right.

Chargebacks

Please contact us first — we're a small team and can almost always resolve issues faster than a bank dispute.
