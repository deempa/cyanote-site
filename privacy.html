Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026

The short version: Cyanote is local-first. Your notes, tasks, habits, and everything you create stay on your own device. We don't run a server that stores your content, we don't have accounts, and there is no analytics or telemetry in the app. The app does make one kind of network request — it checks our update server, and that check carries your licence key. Section 2 explains exactly what that means, including what we can infer from it.

1. What the app collects

Nothing on our servers. All your data — notes, to-dos, calendar, habits, clipboard history, settings — is stored locally on your own computer, in a SQLite database at ~/Library/Application Support/app.cyanote.desktop/ on a Mac and %APPDATA%\app.cyanote.desktop\ on Windows (or a different folder, if you chose one in Settings). It is never uploaded to us and we have no way to read it.

There is no account, and no analytics or telemetry of any kind: no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no usage pings, no device or install identifier generated by the app. The app is also locked down at the browser level — its content security policy permits network connections to exactly one host, updates.cyanote.app, and nowhere else. Everything below is a complete list of what leaves your machine.

2. Software updates — what we can see

Updates are included in the price and cost nothing extra, but the update channel is private: it serves new versions only to requests carrying a valid licence key. This is deliberate — it is what stops the paid app being a free download for anyone who finds the URL — and it has privacy consequences we'd rather state plainly than bury.

Cyanote checks https://updates.cyanote.app/latest.json. That is our own server (a Cloudflare Worker in front of a private storage bucket), not GitHub. Each check sends:

  • Your licence key, in an X-Cyanote-License header. This is a stable identifier tied to your purchase — it does not rotate, and it is the same key that appears on your Lemon Squeezy receipt.
  • Your IP address and the usual HTTP request metadata, as with any web request.

It sends none of your notes or other content. But we want to be direct about the rest: because the key is stable and linked to your purchase, requests to the update server are identifiable as yours. Checks run about 4 seconds after the app launches and then every 6 hours for as long as it stays open, so the server's access logs can be used to derive how often and roughly when you launch and run Cyanote, and from which IP addresses. When you actually install an update, the download request also shows which version you moved to. Request logging is enabled on that service, so those records exist. We don't build profiles from them and we have no interest in doing so — but we could, and a policy that didn't say so would be misleading.

If no licence key is stored, the app does not send the request at all — it skips the check rather than firing one that would be refused. In that state Cyanote makes no network requests whatsoever, and simply won't receive updates.

3. Licence activation

When you enter your licence key in Settings, the app makes one request to https://updates.cyanote.app/activate, sending the key and the fixed label "Cyanote for Mac" (not your computer's name). Our server passes the key to Lemon Squeezy's licence API to activate and validate it, and Lemon Squeezy records the activation against your order. Our server also re-validates keys with Lemon Squeezy when update checks come in, using a short-lived cache so this happens at most a few times a day per key.

The key is stored locally on your own computer. There is no licence check when the app runs — Cyanote works fully without a key; the key only unlocks the update channel.

4. Purchases

Payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller (Merchant of Record). When you buy Cyanote, Lemon Squeezy collects the information needed to complete the sale (e.g. name, email, billing details, and tax location) under their own privacy policy. We receive only limited order information (such as your email and that a purchase was made) to provide support and deliver your download. We never see your full card details.

5. This website

Our website is served by a static hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. We also use privacy-friendly, cookieless analytics to count visits and see which pages are read — it sets no cookies, stores no personal data, and cannot follow you to other sites. We don't add advertising trackers.

6. Support email

If you email us, we'll use your message and address only to help you and to keep a record of the conversation.

7. Your rights

Because we hold almost no personal data, there is little to request — but you can contact us any time to ask what we have, or to have your support correspondence deleted. For purchase data, you can also contact Lemon Squeezy directly.

8. Children

Cyanote is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change and revise the date above.
